Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Application: Paychex QuickBooks Importer

1. Scope

This policy describes the App's intended processing of payroll and QuickBooks Online information when used internally. The App runs on a user-controlled computer; these public legal pages do not host or receive payroll uploads.

2. Information processed

Depending on the files and permissions provided, the App may process employee or payee names, QuickBooks employee identifiers, payroll check numbers, payment dates, net payment amounts, account identifiers, QuickBooks company identifiers, OAuth access and refresh tokens, transaction identifiers, and local import logs. It does not require Social Security numbers or bank account numbers in the Paychex reconciliation spreadsheet.

3. Purposes

Information is used to authenticate access to QuickBooks, identify existing payees and accounting accounts, preview transactions, detect duplicates, create explicitly approved accounting checks, diagnose errors, and maintain an import audit trail.

4. Sources and sharing

Payroll reconciliation data is provided through spreadsheets selected by authorized users. Account, payee, and transaction information is accessed from the authorized QuickBooks company. When users approve posting, the App transmits the necessary transaction details to QuickBooks Online via Intuit APIs. No payroll information is transmitted to this GitHub Pages website. The App is not designed to sell employee data or share it for advertising.

5. Local storage and safeguards

The current intended deployment stores configuration, encrypted OAuth tokens, and operational/import records on the local host computer. Protection depends on the actual deployed configuration, encryption key handling, computer access controls, and backups. Authorized users should restrict access to the host and to spreadsheet exports. Before publishing, the Operator must verify these statements against the exact production build.

6. Retention and deletion

Local logs, authorization tokens, and any retained uploaded data remain subject to the Operator's retention practices and any required business recordkeeping. Authorized administrators can revoke QuickBooks access and remove local tokens; deletion of local data and backups must follow the organization's documented procedures. Contact [PUBLIC PRIVACY EMAIL] to request information about applicable retention and deletion practices.

7. Requests and security incidents

Requests to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to report an apparent security incident, should be sent to [PUBLIC PRIVACY EMAIL]. The Operator will respond as required by applicable law, subject to accounting and employment recordkeeping obligations.

8. Changes and third-party policies

Material changes to processing practices will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date. QuickBooks Online and Paychex are separate services with their own privacy terms.

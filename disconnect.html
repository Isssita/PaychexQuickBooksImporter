Disconnect QuickBooks

You can stop Paychex QuickBooks Importer from accessing your QuickBooks Online company by revoking its authorization.

Revoke access

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online using an account with permission to manage connected applications.
  2. Open the connected applications management area (the location may vary by QuickBooks interface).
  3. Find Paychex QuickBooks Importer or the name under which the integration was authorized.
  4. Select the option to disconnect or revoke access, and confirm the action.
  5. Close the local importer. Ask the application's administrator to remove locally stored OAuth tokens and cached payroll data according to your organization's retention policy.

Important: Revoking authorization stops future API access; it does not automatically delete checks previously posted in QuickBooks or remove locally stored payroll files and logs.

Reconnect later

To reconnect the application, follow the steps on the Connect or Reconnect page.

This page is informational and does not directly call Intuit's token revocation API or delete files.

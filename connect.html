Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks

Paychex QuickBooks Importer is a privately operated, locally hosted tool for reviewing payroll check reports and, after explicit approval, posting check transactions to QuickBooks Online.

Connect from the local application

  1. Open the Paychex QuickBooks Importer application on the authorized computer.
  2. Choose Connect QuickBooks (or the equivalent reconnect option in your installed version).
  3. Sign in through Intuit's authorization page and select the intended QuickBooks company.
  4. Review the access request and approve it only if you recognize the application and the company.
  5. Return to the local application and verify the company and environment before previewing any payroll data.

Security: Never enter Intuit passwords, client secrets, or access tokens into this website. Authentication is completed on Intuit's own authorization pages.

Connection problems?

Reopen the local importer, verify that you are signed into the correct Intuit account, and use its Connect or Reconnect action. Contact your organization's application administrator if access is unavailable.

This page is informational. It does not authorize the application or process OAuth callbacks; those actions are handled by the configured application.
